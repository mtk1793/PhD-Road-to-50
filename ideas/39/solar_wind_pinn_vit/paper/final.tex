\begin{abstract}
We propose a hybrid physics-informed, multi-modal foundation model that combines Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) with Vision Transformer (ViT) backbones and multi-level fusion transformers for real-time solar and wind hybrid forecasting. Key contributions: (1) hybrid loss combining data-driven and physics constraints, (2) temporal-spatial attention for multi-site forecasting, and (3) cross-domain pre-training for improved generalization.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Joint solar and wind forecasting improves grid balancing by leveraging cross-modal information from gridded meteorological fields and local station measurements. Physics-informed losses ensure physically plausible predictions while allowing data-driven flexibility.

\section{Method}
Our prototype uses a ViT-like patch encoder for ERA5-like gridded inputs, a scalar MLP for local station measurements (irradiance and wind speed), and a fusion head producing joint solar and wind power forecasts. Physics losses include a solar irradiance upper bound and a turbine power-curve regularization term.

\section{Experiments}
We evaluate a small prototype on synthetic data designed to mimic irradiance and wind-speed driven generation. The training script emits scatter plots and attention visualizations for model introspection.

\section{Conclusion}
This prototype demonstrates how physics-informed losses and multi-modal fusion can produce reasonable joint forecasts with interpretable attention maps. Future work will replace synthetic data with ERA5/NSRDB/PV-Live/Wind DB and scale the model for production.
