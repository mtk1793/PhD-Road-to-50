\begin{abstract}
We propose a hybrid physics-informed, multi-modal foundation model that combines Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) with Vision Transformer (ViT) backbones and multi-level fusion transformers for real-time solar and wind hybrid forecasting. Key contributions: (1) hybrid loss combining data-driven and physics constraints, (2) temporal-spatial attention for multi-site forecasting, and (3) cross-domain pre-training for transfer to different renewable assets.
\end{abstract}
