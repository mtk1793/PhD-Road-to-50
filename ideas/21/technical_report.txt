## Mathematical Models Implemented

### Governor Model:
ΔṖ_G = (1/T_G)(-ΔP_G + ΔP_c - (1/R)Δf)

### Turbine Model:
ΔṖ_T = (1/T_T)(-ΔP_T + ΔP_G)

### Generator-Load Model:
Δḟ = (1/2H)(ΔP_T + ΔP_wind + ΔP_SMES - ΔP_D - ΔP_tie - DΔf)

### Wind Turbine Model:
P_wind = 0.5 × ρ × A × V³ × Cp(λ,β)

### SMES Model:
ΔE_D = (-K_ID×ΔI_D + K_F×ΔF)/(1+sT_DC)
