Renewable Energy Integration Challenges

Primary Challenges

1. Intermittency & Variability

  • Unpredictable power output from wind and solar
  • Weather-dependent generation patterns
  • High-frequency fluctuations in output

2. Reduced System Inertia

  • Displacement of conventional synchronous generators
  • Lower rotational inertia in the system
  • Faster frequency response required

3. Forecasting Uncertainty

  • Difficulty in accurate generation prediction
  • Planning and scheduling challenges
  • Need for advanced forecasting methods

4. Decentralized Generation

  • Geographically dispersed renewable sources
  • Complex coordination requirements
  • Communication and control challenges
