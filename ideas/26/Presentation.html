Mathematical Models and AGC Strategies

Core Mathematical Models

Swing Equation

2H dΔf/dt = ΔPm - ΔPe - DΔf

Multi-Area Dynamics

dΔfi/dt = (ΔPm,i + ΔPRES,i - ΔPL,i - DiΔfi - ΣTijΔδij)/(2Hi)

Area Control Error

ACEi = ΣΔPtie,ij + BiΔfi

BESS Control

ΔPBESS,i = KP·Δfi + KD·dΔfi/dt + KI∫ACEi dt
