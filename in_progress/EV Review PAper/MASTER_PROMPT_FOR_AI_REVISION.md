#### 6.1 Market Structure Regulations

**FERC Order 2222 (USA, effective 2021, implementation 2021–2025)**
- **Before:** EV aggregators had no market access; revenue model unclear
- **After:** RTOs/ISOs must accept aggregated DER bids in wholesale markets
- **Impact on research:** Shift from utility-managed to competitive aggregation models
- **Status:** Maryland approved V2G-specific regulations (DRIVE Act, June 2025) — first regulatory pathway for EV aggregator wholesale market participation in USA
- **Citation:** FERC documents, Maryland regulatory proceedings, RTO implementation reports (2025)

**EU Alternative Fuels Infrastructure Regulation (AFIR, effective Jan 2026)**
- **Requirement:** All new public charging ≥3.7 kW must support bidirectional capability or V2G-ready architecture
- **Cost impact:** €200–500/charger additional cost; affects 3–5M chargers over 5 years
- **Implication:** V2G becomes commodity infrastructure; research shifts to software/aggregation services
- **Citation:** EU AFIR official text, ACEA industry analysis, standardization committees

#### 6.2 Building Code Integration

**California Title 24 (2023 update)**
- **Requirement:** All new residential construction must have EV charging infrastructure (conduit minimum, Level 2 recommended)
- **Impact:** 100K–200K new charging points/year in California alone
- **Implication:** Residential charging (V2H) becomes standard, not niche
- **Citation:** California Energy Commission, Title 24 specifications, HVAC/EV integration studies

**UK Future Homes Standard (2024)**
- **Requirement:** All new homes EV-ready with energy storage integration
- **Explicit role:** EVs serve as distributed storage for grid flexibility
- **Implication:** Home-level optimization (V2H + PV + HVAC) becomes critical research direction
- **Citation:** UK BEIS documents, Future Homes Standard consultation responses

#### 6.3 Cybersecurity & Privacy Regulations

**EU NIS2 Directive (2024)**
- **Requirement:** Critical infrastructure (incl. EV charging) must meet cybersecurity baseline
- **Standard:** IEC 62443 SL2+ (Industrial Cybersecurity)
- **Impact:** All new chargers after 2026 require security certification
- **Citation:** NIS2 Directive text, ENISA guidance, IEC 62443 implementation

**GDPR + National Data Protection**
- **Impact:** EV charging data (location, behavior, consumption) is personal data requiring protection

- **Implication:** Research must address privacy-by-design, differential privacy, aggregation without central trust
- **Citation:** GDPR text, DPA enforcement actions (Enel 2024 privacy breach case), academic privacy-by-design literature
